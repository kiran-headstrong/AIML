# Monthly Quality Review SOP

Document type: Internal SOP  
Status: Sanitized starter material  
Primary users: Team leads and quality reviewers

## Purpose
This SOP explains how operations leads should prepare the monthly quality review sample and document review notes for recurring process checks.

## Sample selection
Select a balanced sample from the following queues:
- Access requests
- Refund exceptions
- Approval escalations
- Manual account corrections
- Dashboard setup support

The recommended monthly review size is 25 to 40 records for a small operations team.

## Review dimensions
Each sampled item should be checked for:
- Complete intake details
- Correct route or owner assignment
- Clear internal note
- Evidence attachment where required
- Timely escalation when a blocker exists
- No unsupported customer-facing commitment

## Output format
The reviewer should produce a short summary with:
- Total records reviewed
- Number of records with missing information
- Repeat issue themes
- Queue or owner requiring follow-up
- Improvement action for next month

## Escalation
Escalate review findings to the operations lead when the same error pattern appears in three or more records from the same queue.
